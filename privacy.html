Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 | Effective: August 2026 | Friends & Family Beta

FRIENDS & FAMILY BETA - This policy applies to our limited test group.
Your information is used to help you pursue educational opportunities.
It is not the product.

What This App Does

Jasmine Scholarship Hub helps students find scholarships, organize college applications, and get AI-powered writing guidance. It's designed for students age 14 and older.

Information We Collect

Profile Information

  • Name and email address
  • Date of birth (to verify you're 14+)
  • Location (city, state, ZIP code)
  • School and graduation year
  • GPA and academic interests

Documents You Upload

  • Resumes
  • Transcripts and report cards
  • Awards and certificates
  • Scholarship materials

Content You Create

  • Essay drafts
  • Scholarship tracking
  • College lists and goals

How We Use AI

AI helps with:

  • Finding scholarships that match your profile
  • Providing writing feedback and guidance
  • Extracting information from uploaded documents
AI Privacy Protections:
  • We only send AI the minimum information needed for each task
  • AI providers do NOT use your data to train their models (we use zero-retention API settings)
  • AI guides your writing - you remain the author

What We Do NOT Do

  • We do NOT sell your personal information
  • We do NOT use your data for targeted advertising
  • We do NOT share your essays or documents with advertisers
  • We do NOT train AI models on your content

Who Can See Your Information

Who What They Can See
You (the student) Everything in your account
Linked parent/guardian Progress and milestones (if you're under 18 and they're linked)
App administrators Only for troubleshooting, with logging
AI providers Only the specific info needed for each task

Your Rights

  • Access: See all information we have about you
  • Download: Export your data anytime
  • Delete: Delete your account and all data
  • Control: Manage who can see your information

Data Security

  • All data encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
  • Documents stored in private, secure storage
  • Access controls prevent unauthorized viewing

Age Requirements

  • You must be 14 years or older to use this app
  • If you're under 18, a parent/guardian must be notified
  • We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 14

Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data kept while you use the app
  • Deleted accounts: Data removed within 30 days
  • Some logs: Kept up to 1 year for security

Third-Party Services

Service Purpose Data Training?
OpenAI AI writing guidance NO (zero retention)
Vercel App hosting NO

Changes to This Policy

We'll notify you of significant changes through the app. Continued use means you accept updates.
