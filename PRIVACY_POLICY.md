## AI-Powered Features

### How AI Is Used

AI helps:
- Find scholarship opportunities matching your profile
- Analyze scholarship requirements
- Provide writing guidance and feedback
- Evaluate essay strength against rubrics

AI does NOT:
- Write essays for you
- Make application decisions for you
- Share your complete profile with AI providers

### AI Privacy Protections

When AI features are used:
- Only information necessary for the specific task is sent to AI providers
- Personal identifiers are removed or minimized when possible
- AI providers are contractually prohibited from using your data for training
- **You remain the author of your writing**

---

## How We Protect Your Information

### Technical Protections
- All data transmitted using HTTPS/TLS encryption
- Database encryption at rest
- Row-level security restricting access to authorized users only
- Private document storage with access controls
- Secure authentication with email verification

### Access Controls
- Students can only access their own information
- Parents can only access linked student accounts (with student awareness)
- Sharing with counselors requires explicit permission and can be revoked
- Administrative access is logged and restricted

---

## Parent/Guardian Access

For students under 18, a parent or guardian connection is required.

Parents with approved connections may:
- View scholarship and college application progress
- See milestone achievements
- Provide encouragement and optional rewards

Parents cannot:
- Access information without an approved connection
- View content the student has not shared
- Make changes to the student's profile or applications

**What is collected:** Profile information, academic records, application materials, and documents your student uploads.

**Why it's needed:** To match scholarships, organize applications, and provide writing guidance.

**How it's protected:** Encrypted storage, access controls, and database security. Only your student and approved connections can see their information.
